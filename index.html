About Me

Hi, I'm Clover, a postgraduate student at Fudan University with a focus on Rust programming and AI. I'm currently diving deep into the Rust compiler internals and contributing to the Rust ecosystem.

I'm active in the Rust community, primarily on Zulip, where I discuss memory safety, undefined behavior, and compiler design. My open-source work focuses on finding and fixing soundness issues in Rust crates using Miri.

I believe in the power of safe systems programming and am passionate about making Rust's safety guarantees stronger and more accessible.
